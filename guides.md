## Using ITCMon

Notes:

This requires an SDR. See [this page](https://michiganrailfan.github.io/ptc-and-itcmon.html#what-is-an-sdr-which-one-should-i-buy) for info on that. 

Step 1. Download ITCMon [here](github.com/katsojuna/itcmon).

Step 2. UnZIP it.

Step 3. 

<img width="427" height="181" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/e536dac2-54f9-48e9-84ad-3159a3e6fae7" /> 

Type `cmd` in the directory bar in File Explorer and press enter. Then type `mcr.exe -a -d 0 -g 38.5` and press enter.

Open `itcmon.exe` from the folder. You should see things start appearing soon. If not, you may need to check your setup.

## Mapping a Line

You need to drive/walk in some way near the aluminum shed-like structures beside the tracks with a laptop and ITCMon pulled up and an SDR with *no antenna*. Make sure to not trespass or walk on the tracks outside of designated grade crossings. From one edge of the rock to the other is private railroad property so you should not walk on it. You should only see 1 wayside unit at a time and it should be whatever one you are near. You may choose to put coordinates or at least a rough location based on nearby buildings and roads into something like an Excel spreadsheet or Word document or something.
